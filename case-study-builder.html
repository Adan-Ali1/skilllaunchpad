What makes a useful case study?

Give readers enough context to understand the problem, constraints, and your contribution. Show meaningful steps instead of claiming results you cannot verify.

Separate observed outcomes from estimates, protect confidential information, and get permission before sharing client work. This builder organizes a draft; it cannot validate claims or guarantee professional results.
