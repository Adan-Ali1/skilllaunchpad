PRIVACY

Privacy

SkillLaunchpad is an informational website with browser-based tools and an owner-only admin dashboard. Public visitors do not need an account, and public sign-ups are disabled.

Feedback form

If you send feedback, the name, email address, topic, and message you provide are sent through Cloudflare Pages to FormSubmit so the site owner can receive them by email. FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days. Do not include passwords, verification codes, payment details, or sensitive personal information. Read FormSubmit's documentation and privacy notice.

Owner dashboard

The private admin dashboard uses Supabase Auth to sign in the site owner. Visitor sign-ups are disabled. Supabase processes the owner's account email and authentication information to provide this sign-in service; public visitors do not use this account system. See Supabase's privacy notice.

Browser-based tools

The proposal, first-message, and portfolio brief tools generate text in your browser. The information you type is not submitted to SkillLaunchpad or saved by these tools. If you copy a draft, your browser clipboard handles that action. Avoid entering passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Learning progress and portfolio drafts

New roadmaps, practice completion, challenge progress, theme preference, portfolio details, and case-study drafts are stored in local storage in the browser you use. They are not uploaded to SkillLaunchpad for cloud sync. Clearing site data or changing browsers may remove them. Use the dashboard clear-data control to erase learning and portfolio entries from this browser. Avoid entering confidential client material or sensitive personal information.

Optional analytics

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 may measure page views, scrolls, and outbound link clicks. The Google tag is loaded only after you select Accept analytics. If you reject, the tag is not loaded on that visit. Your choice is stored in your browser and you can reopen Privacy choices at any time. If you later reject after accepting, the site updates analytics consent to denied and removes the analytics cookies it can access. Google processes information received through its Analytics service under its own terms and privacy information. Do not enter personal or sensitive information into page URLs or tool fields.

Website hosting and external services

Pages load font files from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google to display the selected typefaces. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which serves the web files and runs the feedback endpoint; hosting providers may process standard technical request information to operate and protect their services. External learning providers and GitHub have their own privacy practices and account requirements. Review their terms before creating an account.

Downloads and changes

Downloadable planners are static text files and do not ask for personal details. If the services or information practices described here change, this page will be updated.
