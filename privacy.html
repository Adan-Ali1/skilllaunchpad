PRIVACY

Privacy

SkillLaunchpad is a static informational website. It has no user accounts, remote contact form, newsletter signup, or advertising. Optional Google Analytics 4 is used only if you select Accept analytics in the privacy-choice panel.

Browser-based tools

The proposal, first-message, and portfolio brief tools generate text in your browser. The information you type is not submitted to SkillLaunchpad or saved by these tools. If you copy a draft, your browser clipboard handles that action. Avoid entering passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Optional analytics

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 may measure page views, scrolls, and outbound link clicks. The Google tag is loaded only after you select Accept analytics. If you reject, the tag is not loaded on that visit. Your choice is stored in your browser and you can reopen Privacy choices at any time. If you later reject after accepting, the site updates analytics consent to denied and removes the analytics cookies it can access. Google processes information received through its Analytics service under its own terms and privacy information. Do not enter personal or sensitive information into page URLs or tool fields.

Website requests

Pages load font files from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google to display the selected typefaces. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which serves the web files; hosting providers may process standard technical request information to operate and protect their services. Consult their current privacy information for details.

Downloads and external links

Downloadable planners are static text files and do not ask for personal details. Learning providers and GitHub are external websites with their own privacy practices and account requirements. Review their terms before creating an account. Do not post passwords, verification codes, private contact details, or payment information in public GitHub feedback.

Changes

If accounts, forms, advertising, or affiliate links are introduced, this page should be updated before those features are enabled.
