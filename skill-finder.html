How the Skill Finder works

Choose the activities you enjoy, the time you can practise, a work style, and something you would like to make. The tool compares your answers with beginner skill paths and shows a few options to explore.

Your answers stay in this browser. You can change direction at any time, and every suggestion is only a place to begin—not a measure of your ability.
