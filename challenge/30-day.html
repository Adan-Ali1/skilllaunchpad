30-Day Skill Practice Challenge

Use a month of small prompts to practise consistently, make a sample, and reflect on what you learned. Adjust the pace or skip days when needed; this is not a test or a promise of employment.

How to use the challenge

Choose a skill, work through one manageable activity at a time, and keep notes about decisions and questions. The goal is a useful learning routine and a project you can explain—not a perfect streak.

Challenge progress is saved in this browser. Make fictional concept work clear, use shareable assets, and avoid putting private information into public samples.
